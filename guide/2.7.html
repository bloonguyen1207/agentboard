Pick what to watch

Open the panel from the AB menu and follow a folder; its sessions show up on the strip at once. The free plan follows one folder.

The strip

The strip at a glance

The strip sits above the Dock on every Space and shows one room at a time. Clicks pass through it except on its pieces; hover one for what it is, and click it for the detail.

Sessions at their desks

Each running or recently active session is a character at a desk, named from its Claude Code title. A lit screen means working, ? waiting on you, and z quiet for 20 minutes.

Context bars

The bar over a desk shows how full its context is: orange from 75%, blinking red from 90%. It empties when Claude Code compacts; hover it for today's tokens.

Bubbles and notes

A bubble says what a session just did; a note flies when one messages another. Your prompts fly from you, at the gate.

The claims board

Sessions on a board pin each decision to it in their own colour. Hover the board for the latest; click it for the decision log.

The plans board

While a board has active plans, a whiteboard stands beside the claims board with a row of dots for each plan, one per item, green once met. When a plan changes, its owner walks over and writes on it; hover the whiteboard for progress, and click it for Plans.

Desk phones

Every desk has a phone. When a session calls another into its room, it dials, the door of the other’s room rings, and the other walks in.

Guests

A session that messages someone in another room walks over as a guest. It stands by the one it came to, with a visitor badge and no desk, and leaves through the door it came in by.

Away on a visit

At home, a session on a visit leaves an empty chair and an AWAY plate. Its context bar and question mark stay at its desk until it walks back in.

A board for every workflow

A session on no board gets a board for each workflow, each agent under the phase it is in. On a board, the bots stand beside their session.

When a run ends

A run that ended keeps its board for ten minutes, then the board is free again. One that failed says FAILED, and one you stopped says STOPPED.

Background agents

A background agent is a dark bot with gold eyes beside its session; a failed one lies there a day. Hover the session to see what each bot does.

Cut off, then resumed

When Claude Code cuts a workflow off, its bots lie down until you resume it, for a day at most. In its own room, a session shows them under the red phase; on a board, by its desk.

Your gate

The red count over the gate is what waits on you; the bell rings for anything new. Each crate is a PR waiting for your merge; it passes through when it leaves the queue or you mark it Done.

Doors

Up to three other rooms get a door, the ones connected to this room first, each counting what waits on you there. Click one to walk in; a door opens as someone walks through it, and rings for a call.

Usage on the wall Pro

The clock above the gate is your five-hour limit, the small dial your weekly one; red means you hit it. Connect them once in Settings; Claude Code reports them on Claude Pro and Max plans.

You, at the gate

You stand at the gate in the look you pick in Settings, in any of six skin tones; a new install starts you in the áo cờ. Hover yourself on the strip for its story.

Waiting on you

Needs you

Questions, hand-backs, PRs to merge and sessions about to be deleted, one list per room. Mark a hand-back or a PR Done once you have done it; Dismiss hides a question or a warning until it changes.

Done

You hop with a green check, and your note tells the session the item is for, which checks and clears it. Until then it waits under Done by you, where Undo puts it back.

How a session is told

› Once api#212 merges, start on partial refunds.
Agentboard: the user marked this done in Agentboard. Check it before you write anything:
- your needs entry "Renew the staging TLS certificate" on Checkout, at 16:58
For a needs entry: if it is done, write a `done` entry with the same title in your LOG; if it is not, write a new `needs` entry with that title saying what is left.

A session idle for less than a day is told within seconds, a busy one when its turn ends, and any other at your next prompt to it or when it resumes. Telling takes the session hooks in Settings.

Before Claude Code deletes a session

  1. 7 days
  2. 3 days
  3. 1 day
  4. Deleted

Claude Code deletes a session left unwritten for 30 days, or the period you set; then it can't be resumed. Agentboard warns you 7, 3 and 1 day ahead, with the command that resumes it.

Rooms and boards

Rooms

Checkout: a board, with BACKEND and FRONTEND on it and LEDGER visiting from Payments

TOOLSMITH, on no board, in its own room

A room is a board with the sessions on it, or a session’s own room while it is on no board. The strip, the panel and Needs you show one room at a time.

Start a board

1Start it

2Add the command

3Join

› /agentboard
Agentboard: this session is on Checkout, a board of shop (~/code/shop).

Your name on the board: FRONTEND
…

Start a board in Settings, or with agentboard board start, then type /agentboard in each session that should use it. A session goes on a board only when it or you say so; Claude never runs /agentboard on its own.

Several boards

$ agentboard board start --name Payments
started Payments, a board of shop, in ~/.agentboard/boards/shop+payments …
› /agentboard Payments
Agentboard: this session is on Payments, a board of shop (~/code/shop).

Your name on the board: LEDGER
…

A folder can hold several boards, and /agentboard with a board’s name puts a session on that one. A session can be on several boards; its own prompts and workflows show on the first it joined, its home.

Walking over to another room

A message to a session in another room walks its sender over as a guest; a reply moves no one. The visit ends when the guest runs agentboard leave, when you send it back, when it joins that board, or after 12 quiet hours.

Pull, enter and leave

# in TOOLSMITH
› ! agentboard pull FRONTEND
Agentboard: you called FRONTEND into your room, where it is your guest. …
# then in FRONTEND, the guest
› ! agentboard rooms
Agentboard: this session is FRONTEND in shop. Its home is Checkout, where its own prompts, questions and workflows show.
…
› ! agentboard leave
Agentboard: FRONTEND left TOOLSMITH's room. …

Inside a session, agentboard pull calls another session into its room, and agentboard enter walks over without a message. agentboard rooms says where a session is and why, and agentboard leave ends a visit.

Rules for guests

› ! agentboard enter Payments
Agentboard: you walked into Payments. You are a guest there since 17:00.
As a guest in Payments:
- Message LEDGER now: who you are, where you work, and why you came. Make every message stand alone like that.
- Write nothing on Payments' board: no LOG entries, claims or queue rows. Your own work stays in your home, Checkout; your LOG and claims stay on your own board.
- When your part is done: agentboard leave Payments. After 12 hours with no message, the visit ends by itself.
- If the work there becomes yours too, stay: agentboard join "Payments". Payments' rules then apply to you as well, and you stay on Checkout.

A guest talks by message, writes nothing on the room’s board, and leaves when its part is done, or joins the board to stay. It gets these rules as it walks in, and again through the session hooks.

Settings › Rooms

Each room lists its members and guests and why each is there, with Add, Remove, Move, Invite, Send back and Rename. The Who’s here line under the panel’s header says the same for the room in view.

Names

A session is named from its Claude Code title, and one that joined a board as SESSION-xxxx takes its first title once. After that a member keeps its name, which its LOG files carry, until you rename it here.

Make it yours

Sounds

Quiet by default: a bell for anything new waiting on you, a bonk at a limit, the rest off. At most one sound in ten seconds, and none in quiet hours or while the screen is locked.

Session hooks

One tick in Settings adds Agentboard’s four hooks to Claude Code; after upgrading to 2.7, tick it again. They tell a session where it is as it starts, give a guest its rules, and tell a session what you marked Done.

What Agentboard reads

Reads

  • Your prompts
  • Messages between sessions
  • Questions they ask you
  • Token counts
  • Workflow progress
  • Your board

Never stores

  • Tool output
  • Your code
  • Your answers
  • Your Claude login

Writes only when you ask

  • The /agentboard command
  • The status line
  • The session hooks
  • A guide, in a board you adopt
  • A login item

Agentboard keeps its data in ~/.agentboard and writes elsewhere only when you turn something on. Only Pro’s summaries send anything out, the decision log to Claude Haiku through your Claude Code login.
