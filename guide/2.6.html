Pick what to watch

Open the panel from the AB menu and follow a folder; its sessions show up on the strip at once. Free follows one folder, Pro every folder.

The strip

The strip at a glance

The strip sits above the Dock on every Space; clicks pass through it except on its pieces. Hover a piece for what it is, and click it for the detail.

Sessions at their desks

Each running or recently active session is a character at a desk, named from its Claude Code title. A lit screen means working, ? waiting on you, and z quiet for 20 minutes.

Context bars

The bar over a desk shows how full its context is: orange from 75%, blinking red from 90%. It empties when Claude Code compacts; hover it for today's tokens.

Bubbles and notes

A bubble says what a session just did; a note flies when one messages another. Your prompts fly from you, at the gate.

The claims board

Sessions on a board pin each decision to it in their own colour. Hover the board for the latest; click it for the decision log.

A board for every workflow

A session working alone gets a board for each workflow, each agent under the phase it is in. In a crew, the bots stand beside their session.

Background agents

A background agent is a dark bot with gold eyes beside its session; a failed one lies there a day. Hover the session to see what each bot does.

Cut off, then resumed

When Claude Code cuts a workflow off, its bots lie down until you resume it, for a day at most. Working alone, a session shows them under the red phase; in a crew, by its desk.

Your gate

The red count over the gate is what waits on you; the bell rings for anything new. Each crate is a PR waiting for your merge; it passes through when it leaves the queue.

Doors

The three busiest other crews get a door, each counting what waits on you there. Click one to walk in; a quiet strip walks to a busy one by itself.

Usage on the wall Pro

The clock above the gate is your five-hour limit, the small dial your weekly one; red means you hit it. Connect them once in Settings; Claude Code reports them on Claude Pro and Max plans.

You, at the gate

You stand at the gate in the look you pick in Settings, in any of six skin tones. Hover yourself on the strip for its story.

Waiting on you

Needs you

Questions, hand-backs, PRs to merge and sessions about to be deleted, one list per crew. Items clear on their own when done; Dismiss hides one until it changes.

Before Claude Code deletes a session

  1. 7 days
  2. 3 days
  3. 1 day
  4. Deleted

Claude Code deletes a session left unwritten for 30 days, or the period you set; then it can't be resumed. Agentboard warns you 7, 3 and 1 day ahead, with the command that resumes it.

Sessions in a folder that work together are a crew, with its own strip, conversation and Needs you. A session working alone is a crew of one.

Start a board from the panel, then type /agentboard in each session that should use it. The board lives in ~/.agentboard, outside your repo; Claude never runs /agentboard on its own.

Leave a crew

› ! agentboard leave
Agentboard: FRONTEND left shop. …

Type ! agentboard leave in a session, or click Leave in Settings, to take it off its crew. A new message with the crew brings it back.

What Agentboard reads

Reads

  • Your prompts
  • Messages between sessions
  • Questions they ask you
  • Token counts
  • Workflow progress
  • Your board

Never stores

  • Tool output
  • Your code
  • Your answers
  • Your Claude login

Writes only when you ask

  • The /agentboard command
  • The status line
  • The session-start reminder
  • A guide, in a board you adopt
  • A login item

Agentboard keeps its data in ~/.agentboard and writes elsewhere only when you turn something on. Only Pro's summaries send anything out: the decision log goes to Claude Haiku through your Claude Code login.
